Privacy Policy

Cortex / Nexus Management Network
Effective: September 2026
Summary: We only access your public Roblox User ID and Username via OAuth 2.0 to authenticate your membership. We never collect passwords, private messages, or billing details.
1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Cortex / Nexus Management System ("Nexus501", "we", "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information when users authenticate and interact with our operational management platform.

2. Information We Collect via Roblox OAuth 2.0

When you sign in using Log In with Roblox, we request access exclusively to standard identity scopes (openid and profile). Through these scopes, we retrieve:

  • Roblox User ID: To verify your identity and group membership.
  • Roblox Username & Display Name: To display your callsign and link activity logs to your profile.
  • Avatar Thumbnail URL: To display your operative card within the roster.

What we DO NOT collect: We cannot and do not collect your Roblox password, email address, payment information, personal location, or private communication.

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used solely to:

  • Verify your authorization tier (Operative, Staff, or High Command).
  • Maintain group activity records, training points, and mission completion logs.
  • Enable self-service access to your personal quota and progress dashboards.
4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or distribute your information to any third parties or commercial entities. All data remains strictly within the operational sector database.

5. Data Retention & Revocation

You may revoke access to the Nexus501 application at any time directly through your Roblox Account Settings → Authorized Applications. Upon revocation, your access token is immediately invalidated.

6. Contact & Inquiries

For data inquiries, access questions, or deletion requests, please contact Sector Command via the official Taskforce Discord communications server.
